The dual track
Staff / Principal and Manager are peers at L4–L5. Neither is above the other. Staying is a valid career.
Governance non-negotiables
Risk veto + board access · one accountable owner per decision · G2/G3 gates · independent validation · blameless SEV-1/2 post-mortems.
Compensation shape
L1 = 1.00 index. L8 ≈ 13–26×. PnL share and deferral above L4. Risk-adjusted, never raw P&L.
How to use it
Pick a track, locate the band, read the mission and gate. Use the full framework for KPIs, hiring loops and RACI.
